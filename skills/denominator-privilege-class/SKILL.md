# Denominator-Privilege Class (DPC)

Research-stage classification. Not a physical discovery.

## Rule

A published quantity is granted the privilege of being a count of real
events. The denominator is effort, completeness, area, or discoverable-set
treated as observed. Changing it rewrites history without objects moving.

## Kill

The pipeline already treats the denominator as a first-class estimand with
its own error. Nested rewrite inside 2% also kills.

## Do not re-issue

OAC, FPC, PDC, ANC, DCC, IPC, UPRC.

## Leftover (do not mint here)

Remainder-Growth Class: effort grows unnamed remainder faster than the
named set. Different kill rule (saturation).
